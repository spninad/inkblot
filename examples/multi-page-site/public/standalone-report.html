Riemannian Trajectory Planners: Geometry-Aware Score Guidance in Continuous Action Spaces

October 2026

Abstract

Autonomous embodied decision-making requires synthesizing kinodynamically feasible trajectories through complex non-convex environments. While standard diffusion planners generate coherent plans in unconstrained Euclidean domains, they routinely violate physical manifolds and obstacle boundaries when projected into task space. In this report, we formulate diffusion trajectory planning over Riemannian manifolds $(\mathcal{M}, g)$ equipped with position-dependent metric tensors induced by obstacle signed distance fields (SDF). We introduce Geodesic Score Guidance (GSG), proving asymptotic convergence to collision-free geodesics under test-time classifier-free guidance. Across continuous manipulation and locomotion benchmarks, our method improves task completion by +28.7% over standard Euclidean diffusion planners while reducing sample variance by 64%.
